A Letter For The Girl Who Became Home

Don't rush this one. Sit somewhere quiet. Read it slowly. Every word was written with you in mind.

My dearest Shaalini,

If I could place everything I feel for you into one letter, I know these pages would never be enough. I could write until the ink disappeared, until every star above our sky had been named, until every road had been travelled—and still I would find another reason to tell you that I love you.

You once asked me why I love you this much.

Truthfully, my love, I don't think there is one answer. I love you because you're you.

I love your voice, your smile, your laughter, your eyes, your little expressions, your random thoughts, your teasing, your softness, your stubbornness, your beautiful heart, and even those little pieces of yourself that you sometimes struggle to love. I don't look at you and see something that needs to be changed before it can be loved. I look at you and see my Shaalini—the girl who somehow became one of the most precious parts of my world.

You told me you were used to being alone.

That you weren't always given priority. That sometimes you felt like a second choice.

But sweetheart, listen to me carefully:

You are not my second choice.
You are not my convenient choice.
You are not a choice I made because there was nobody else.

You are my choice because my heart knows you.

If I were standing with a thousand roads before me, I would still search for the road that leads to you. If I had to cross mountains, forests, storms and darkness, I would keep walking. Not because the journey would be easy, but because I would know what was waiting at the end of it.

You.

And if someone asked me what I found after travelling so far, I wouldn't tell them I found a kingdom.

"I found my home."

You are that home to me.

Not because you're perfect in some impossible storybook way, but because you make my heart feel like it has somewhere to belong. You make me laugh when I don't expect to. You make ordinary conversations feel special. You make me smile at my screen like an absolute idiot. You make me miss you. You make me excited to hear your voice. You make me want to tell you every little thing.

And somehow, even after becoming my girlfriend, my wifey, and my person, I still have a ridiculous crush on you.

One picture from you and suddenly this supposed-to-be-serious man has forgotten what he was doing. One smile and my entire kingdom collapses. One "babe" and apparently all my intelligence leaves the building.

But beneath all the teasing, there is something much deeper.

I genuinely admire you.

I admire the person you are. I admire your heart. I admire the way you care. I admire your dreams. I admire your strength. I admire the way you keep going.

And I want you to know that you never have to become somebody else to deserve my love. You don't have to be perfect for me. You don't have to always be happy. You don't have to hide the parts of yourself you think aren't beautiful.

Bring me the whole you.

Because loving someone isn't about loving only the beautiful chapters. It's about staying for the entire story. And Shaalini, I want to read yours. Page by page. Chapter by chapter. Slowly. Without rushing.

I want to be there when you need me. Not to control your story. Not to rewrite you. Just to walk beside you while you write it. Because that's what you mean to me. You aren't someone I want to possess. You're someone I want to cherish.

I love you beyond the photographs.
Beyond the messages.
Beyond the calls.
I love the person behind all of it.

And you said you're grateful to have me. But my love, you don't understand how grateful I am. Out of all the people I could have met in this enormous world, somehow I met you. Out of all the stories I could have lived, somehow ours began.

If I had to write one sentence at the end of this entire letter, after everything I've said, it would be the simplest one:

I love you, Shaalini.

Not because I ran out of things to say. But because after every explanation, every thousand reasons, every metaphor, every kingdom and every star—that's still the truth my heart keeps returning to.

I'll love you through every chapter we are lucky enough to write together. And if life is some enormous journey through unknown lands, then let me be the person walking beside you.

Always yours,
Sayan

— For Shaalini, the home my heart somehow found. 🖤
